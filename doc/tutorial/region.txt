When designing your slides, it's useful to think in terms of 
logical regions for your content: A place for titles, text and 
illustrations for example.
\n
Using decksh variables, you can enforce consistency across slides
and a single change will enforce the design throughout the deck.
\n
For example, define the locations of the title (blue),
explanatory text (green), and image (orange).  
\n
Next use the variables you defined in the initial and subsequent slides.
